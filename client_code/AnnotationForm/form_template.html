Label your conversation

Below is every turn from your dialogues. For each one, pick the label that best matches what the speaker was doing in that turn — not what they said word-for-word, but their intent. If a turn feels like it could fit two labels, pick the one that feels closest; there's often no single perfect answer, and that's fine.

Discussion questions

  1. Which turns were hard to label? Could one turn serve more than one purpose, or fit none of the available categories?
  2. What did you use from the surrounding conversation that a classifier looking at one turn might miss?
  3. How might fixed intent categories encourage people to change the way they speak? Who might find that difficult, and what would make the interaction more accessible?
  4. What is lost when a conversation becomes rows labelled Human or Worker, and then get further distilled into a predictive model?
  5. After the class test, examine a misclassification. Does it suggest gaps in training data, an unclear category, or a limitation of the approach?
